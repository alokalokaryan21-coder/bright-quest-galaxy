MOONBERRY PLAY LAB — PUBLIC WEBSITE (CLOUDFLARE PAGES)
========================================================
This is a NEW, separate public website. Your original Cheeku's Playful Galaxy
site is not changed by these files.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Public homepage, four interactive playable games, parent birthday service page,
  About, Privacy, Terms, 404 page, and mobile-friendly responsive design.
- No analytics scripts, advertising scripts, payment forms, children's details,
  accounts, or email collection have been added to this package.

BEFORE YOU ACCEPT PAID INQUIRIES
1. Add YOUR BUSINESS email address to assets/config.js:
   window.MOONBERRY_CONFIG = { contactEmail: 'hello@example.com' };
   Currently the inquiry button is disabled to avoid showing a non-working form.
2. Review package prices, scope, privacy policy, and terms against your actual
   business practices and local laws. These are working templates, not legal advice.
3. If you wish to keep customers' personalized birthday pages private, implement
   actual access controls rather than relying on an unlisted URL.
4. Handle payments and personal data directly with a parent/guardian, and agree
   in writing on site removal and handling of children's photos/details.

DEPLOY AS A SEPARATE CLOUDFLARE PAGES SITE
1. Visit Cloudflare Dashboard > Workers & Pages > Create application > Pages.
2. Choose Upload assets (direct upload) and create a NEW project, such as
   moonberry-play-lab. Do not choose your existing Cheeku Pages project.
3. Upload the ZIP file provided, or drag in the contents of this unzipped folder.
   index.html must be at the ROOT (not inside an extra directory).
4. Deploy, then open the generated https://<project>.pages.dev URL.
5. Test /memory/, /maths/, /words/, /robot/, /parents/, /privacy/.
6. Later, attach a custom domain if you want; it is not required for a public launch.

ANALYTICS / ADSENSE
- Cloudflare Web Analytics can be enabled from your project dashboard. The site
  does not inject any tracker by default.
- Do not insert AdSense immediately. This is child-directed content. Verify age-
  appropriate ad settings, applicable child privacy requirements, ad platform
  eligibility, and site-content quality before applying or inserting ad scripts.
- No guarantee of AdSense eligibility, approval or earnings.
