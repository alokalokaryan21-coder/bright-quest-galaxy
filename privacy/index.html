Privacy and safety

How this website works and what families should know.

Effective: November 22, 2026

Our free games

The games in this version of Moonberry Play Lab run in your browser. You can play without creating an account. We do not ask children to provide names, email addresses, birthdays or photos to play the games. Word Trails uses browser storage to remember puzzle progress and daily challenge history on this browser. The game does not send this information to us. Clearing this site’s browser data removes the saved progress and history.

Website hosting

The site is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Like other hosting providers, Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses and request information for website delivery, security, and operational purposes, in accordance with its policies.

Traffic analytics

The owner may enable Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits and page performance. We do not currently embed personalized advertising or third-party advertising trackers in this package. If analytics or advertising technologies are changed, this notice must be reviewed and updated before deployment.

Parent inquiries

Birthday service inquiries are intended for adults only. When configured, the inquiry form opens your own email application; it does not submit information directly to the website. A parent may choose what to write and send. Please do not send a child's personal information in an initial inquiry.

Personalized birthday websites

Personalized birthday websites may require separate privacy and access arrangements agreed with the parent. Families should not assume that a hard-to-guess public URL is private. We recommend permission-based access and an agreed deletion date for personal material.

Children's information

We do not intentionally solicit information directly from children. Parents should supervise younger children using online services and contact the site operator if they have concerns.

Contact and updates

The site operator should add a business contact method before accepting inquiries. This draft privacy notice must be checked against the website's actual configuration and applicable laws before launch. We may update it when our services change.
