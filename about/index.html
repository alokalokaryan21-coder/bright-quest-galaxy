Our little story

Learning feels brighter when it feels like play.

Moonberry Play Lab is a small, independent project creating friendly browser games for kids and families.

What we make

Our games offer short, welcoming challenges for memory, vocabulary, number fluency and logical thinking. They're intended for curious young learners and their families—not as formal educational assessments.

Our approach

Free play should be easy to access. The current games do not require a login, ask children for their names, or store game scores on our server. We aim to keep the experience simple and lighthearted.

For parents

We're also exploring parent-commissioned birthday mini-websites, using a reusable template to create personal celebrations. You can see the proposed packages on our Parents page.

Have feedback?

Please ask a parent or guardian to get in touch through the contact information on our Parents page once inquiries open. We welcome ideas for safe, helpful games.
